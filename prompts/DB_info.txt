Database Overview:
- This database contains 4,641 entries of bimetallic alloy catalysts
- Each entry represents a potential adsorption site for hydrogen evolution reaction (HER)
- The data is used for pre-DFT screening of catalyst performance

Database Structure:
The database has 5 columns with the following information:


1. Metal1 (Primary Metal):
   - The main metal component of the alloy
   - Possible candidates: ['Cu', 'Ir', 'Pd', 'Pt', 'Ag', 'Re', 'Rh', 'Os', 'W', 'Mo', 'Au', 'Hf', 'Ru', 'Zr', 'Zn', 'Nb', 'Ti', 'Tc', 'Y', 'Sc', 'V', 'Ta', 'Cr', 'La', 'Co', 'Cd', 'Ni', 'Fe', 'Hg', 'Mn']
   - This is the metal atom that adsorbates first encounter

2. Metal2 (Secondary Metal):
   - The secondary metal mixed with the primary metal
   - Possible candidates: ['Cu', 'Ir', 'Pd', 'Pt', 'Ag', 'Re', 'Rh', 'Os', 'W', 'Mo', 'Au', 'Hf', 'Ru', 'Zr', 'Zn', 'Nb', 'Ti', 'Tc', 'Y', 'Sc', 'V', 'Ta', 'Cr', 'La', 'Co', 'Cd', 'Ni', 'Fe', 'Hg', 'Mn']
   - Forms the bimetallic alloy with Metal1

3. Metal1_Composition:
   - Molar fraction of Metal1 in the alloy
   - Possible number: 0.75, 0.50

4. Metal2_Composition:
   - Molar fraction of Metal2 in the alloy
   - Must equal (1 - Metal1_Composition)
   - Possible number: 0.25, 0.50

5. Site (Adsorption Site Geometry):
   - The geometric location where adsorption occurs
   - Three possible values:
     * "top": Adsorption atop a single metal atom
     * "bridge": Adsorption between two adjacent metal atoms
     * "hollow": Adsorption within the triangle formed by three metal atoms

Data Validation Rules:
- Metal1_Composition + Metal2_Composition must equal 1.0
- Site must be one of: "top", "bridge", or "hollow"
- All compositions must be between 0.00 and 1.00

Usage Instructions:
1. Filter the database to find specific alloy combinations
2. Consider different adsorption sites for each alloy
3. Use this data to identify promising catalysts for HER
4. The goal is to find catalysts with H adsorption energy closest to zero

Example Queries:
- Find all Pt-based alloys
- Find catalysts with specific composition ratios
- Find catalysts with specific adsorption sites
- Combine multiple criteria for targeted search

This database is your primary resource for identifying and analyzing bimetallic catalyst candidates for hydrogen evolution reaction optimization.
