- The “noble dilution” lever (diluting Pt with Au or Ag at 75:25) is very robust across top/bridge/hollow — Pt–Au and Pt–Ag at 0.75:0.25 consistently produce near-target ΔG_H.

Revised primary hypothesis (concise)
- Mixing a hydrogen-strong host with a noble or more noble partner remains a valid tuning strategy, but the effect is highly pair-specific and controlled by local ensembles (which depend on site and composition). In practice:
  - Pt diluted with Au or Ag at 75:25 is a robust path to ΔG_H ≈ −0.27 eV across sites.
  - Cu, W, and Co partners can produce either extreme weakening or strengthening depending strongly on site and composition — they are powerful but non-monotonic levers (i.e., small composition/site changes can flip ΔG_H sign).
