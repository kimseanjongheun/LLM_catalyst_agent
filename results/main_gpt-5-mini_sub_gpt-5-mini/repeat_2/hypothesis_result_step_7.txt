가설 수정 결과: Revised concise hypothesis:

Noble–non‑noble bimetallics can achieve near‑optimal ΔE_H ≈ −0.27 eV, but only inside narrow, pair‑, composition‑, adsorption‑site‑ and surface‑layer‑specific windows. Concretely, noble‑rich (~75:25) hollow‑site configurations of Pd–Ni and Ir–Fe (ΔE_H ≈ −0.25 to −0.26 eV; Ni–Pd shows the same window when ordering is swapped) lie within that target range, whereas changing site or ordering (e.g., Pd–Ni@bridge) shifts ΔE_H by ≥~0.15 eV toward weaker binding. Exposing early transition‑metal partners at the surface produces severe overbinding (examples: V‑surface in V–Pt gives ΔE_H ≈ −0.99 eV; Ag–Re and Ag–Mo ≲ −0.6 eV), while some pairs underbind (Cu–Co, Au–Ti > 0 eV). Therefore, reaching ΔE_H ≈ −0.27 eV requires pair‑specific mapping of composition × site × ordering (not blanket noble enrichment); modest composition/site tuning (e.g., Au–W 50:50 vs 75:25) can shift ΔE_H by ~0.25 eV and recover acceptable behavior.
