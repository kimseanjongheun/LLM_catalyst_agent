가설 수정 결과: Revised concise hypothesis: Noble–non‑noble alloying can reach the target ΔE_H ≈ −0.27 eV, but success is highly pair-, composition- and site-specific (e.g., Pd–Ni(0.75:0.25)@hollow ≈ −0.253 eV and Pt–V(0.75:0.25)@bridge is also promising), while early‑TM partners (Mo, Re, W) tend to strong overbinding — therefore systematic per‑pair mapping of site and composition windows (not uniform noble‑enrichment assumptions) is required.
