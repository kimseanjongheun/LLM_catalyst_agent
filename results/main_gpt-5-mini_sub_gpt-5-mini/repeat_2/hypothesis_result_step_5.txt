가설 수정 결과: Revised concise hypothesis

Noble–non‑noble alloying can achieve ΔE_H ≈ −0.27 eV, but only within very narrow, pair‑, composition‑, site‑ and surface‑layer‑specific windows. The present data confirm Pd–Ni and Ir–Fe (noble‑rich compositions with H at hollow sites) are near‑optimal (Pd–Ni(0.75:0.25)@hollow = −0.2528 eV; Ir–Fe(0.75:0.25)@hollow = −0.2624 eV). Pt–V is highly configuration‑dependent and asymmetric: Pt‑rich configurations only approach ~−0.12 to −0.17 eV, whereas V‑dominated/hollow arrangements produce extreme overbinding (V‑Pt(0.75:0.25)@hollow = −0.9927 eV), showing that which element occupies the surface layer (ordering) crucially controls ΔE_H. Early‑TM partners (Mo, Re, W) generally overbind with coinage/noble metals, though particular composition/site choices (e.g., Au–W 0.50:0.50@top = −0.1419 eV) can mitigate overbinding. Therefore, further work must map composition, adsorption site and element‑ordering for each pair individually rather than assuming uniform noble enrichment.
