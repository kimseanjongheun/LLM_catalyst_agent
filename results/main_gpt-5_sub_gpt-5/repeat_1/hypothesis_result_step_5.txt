가설 수정 결과: Revised hypothesis (v5) based on the latest simulation data

What held
- Pd- and 3d–Cu systems: Non-hollow sites can access coinage-rich ensembles that drastically weaken H binding (often unbound). Hollow remains the only viable site for Pd–Ag and Pd–Cu; Ni/Co–Cu show very large site-induced weakening.
- Pt hollow-site diluent order (hollow only) persists: Cu (weakest) > Au > Ag (strongest).
- Rh–Cu: Moderate hollow→bridge weakening (~0.1 eV); site switching cannot reach −0.27 at 0.75.

What changed (key corrections)
- Site ordering is not universal. Two clear exceptions emerge at 0.75/0.25:
  - Pt–Au: top binds stronger than hollow and bridge (−0.2787 < −0.2532 < −0.2265 eV), breaking the “hollow > bridge > top” rule.
  - Pt–Cu: bridge binds stronger than hollow (−0.2334 < −0.1527), but top is extremely weak/unbound (+0.7323), yielding a non-monotonic ladder.
- Pt host “small-shift” behavior holds in magnitude but not in direction across sites:
  - Pt–Ag remains gently weakening hollow→bridge→top (total Δh→t = +0.0226 eV).
  - Pt–Au shows small but non-monotonic shifts: hollow→bridge weakens (+0.0267 eV) yet bridge→top strengthens (−0.0522 eV), net hollow→top strengthening (−0.0255 eV).
- Pd–Cu confirms the Pd+coinage rule: large non-hollow weakening (h→b = +0.070 eV; b→t = +0.603 eV; h→t = +0.673 eV).
- Rh–Ag top remains unavailable in the DB; no site-based rescue path is present.

Refined working model
- Alloy- and ensemble-specific site sensitivity governs direction and magnitude:
  - Pt hosts: Site shifts are small in magnitude (~0.02–0.08 eV per step) but can be non-monotonic. Top can either gently weaken (Pt–Ag) or gently strengthen (Pt–Au) relative to hollow, depending on the diluent and the accessible local ensemble.
  - Pd + coinage (Ag/Cu): Non-hollow sites preferentially sample coinage-rich ensembles that severely destabilize H; hollow is the only viable site.
  - 3d–Cu (Ni/Co–Cu): Non-hollow sites produce very large, non-linear weakening; not site-tunable near −0.27 at 0.75.
  - Rh systems: Modest site shifts, but DB lacks the needed top/bridge entries to rescue Rh–Ag; Rh–Cu remains slightly weak at hollow with no site lever at 0.75.
- Practical guidance (current best performers at 0.75/0.25):
  - Pt–Ag top: −0.2799 eV (adopt).
  - Pt–Au top: −0.2787 eV (adopt; update from prior hollow recommendation).
  - Pd–Ag hollow: −0.2911 eV (acceptable; avoid site switching).
  - Pt–Au hollow: −0.2532 eV (acceptable if slightly weaker binding is desired).

How these results refine the hypothesis
- Site-ordering rule is conditional: it holds for Pd–Ag, Pd–Cu, Ni/Co–Cu, and Rh–Cu, but breaks for Pt–Au (top strengthens) and partially for Pt–Cu (bridge strengthens; top destabilizes strongly). Thus, for Pt hosts, top can serve as a fine-tuning lever in either direction depending on the coinage partner.
- We codify: For Pt–Au and Pt–Ag at 25% diluent, the top site is the working site near −0.28 eV; for Pd–coinage, hollow is the only viable site; for 3d–Cu, non-hollow sites are detrimental; for Rh–Ag, current DB lacks a viable site lever.
