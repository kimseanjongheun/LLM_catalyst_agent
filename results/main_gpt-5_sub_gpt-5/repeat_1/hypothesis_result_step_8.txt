Refined working model
- Site sensitivity is highly alloy- and ensemble-specific with strong host/diluent asymmetry.
  - Pt hosts:
    - Ag/Au: modest, sometimes non-monotonic shifts; top can slightly strengthen vs hollow and sit near −0.28. Composition offers fine-tuning.
    - Cu/3d: hollow tends to be too weak; non-hollow can be severely destabilized (tops may become unbound). Avoid for HER near −0.27.
  - Pd + coinage: coinage-rich non-hollow ensembles drastically destabilize H. Hollow is the only viable site; modest composition tuning around Pd–Ag can reach the target.
  - Cu hosts:
    - Co diluent is an effective composition lever in Cu-rich regime at hollow (≈−0.27 achievable). Non-hollow is strongly weakened (bridge/top poor).
    - Ni diluent over-weakens hollow as Cu increases; Cu-rich Cu–Ni is unfavorable.
  - 3d host + Cu diluent (Ni–Cu, Co–Cu at 0.75 3d): hollow overbinds while non-hollow over-weakens; not site-tunable; composition must flip to Cu-host side.
  - Rh systems: hollow with coinage is too strong; with Cu slightly weak at Rh0.75; with Ni too strong. Lack of alternative sites in DB limits tuning; only composition remains.
  - Ru–Cu: Ru-rich hollow overbinds; expect Cu enrichment to move ΔG_H* toward −0.27, with non-hollow likely too weak.
