Refined working model
- Site sensitivity remains ensemble-specific with strong host/diluent asymmetry.
  - Pt hosts with coinage: top-site tuning near −0.27 remains the most promising.
  - Pd + coinage: only hollow is viable; Pd–Ag can be tuned slightly toward −0.27 by reducing Pd fraction.
  - Cu hosts: Co uniquely strengthens hollow toward −0.27; Ni/Fe/Mn over‑weaken hollow as Cu increases.
  - 3d host + Cu: hollow overbinds while non-hollow over-weakens; not site-tunable.
  - Rh systems: hollows often too strong (with coinage, Ni) or slightly weak (with Cu); composition is the only lever.
  - Ru–Cu: crossover from overbinding to underbinding is sharp and asymmetric; focus on Ru‑host compositions around Ru 0.60–0.70.
