Refined working model
- Site sensitivity is ensemble-specific with strong host/diluent asymmetry.
  - Pt hosts: coinage (Ag/Au) yields tunable, near-target tops; 3d diluents (Cu/Ni) weaken hollow and catastrophically destabilize non-hollow.
  - Pd + coinage: only hollow is viable; modest composition tuning around Pd–Ag can approach −0.27.
  - Cu hosts: Co is uniquely effective to strengthen hollow toward −0.27 in Cu‑rich alloys; Ni/Fe/Mn over‑weaken hollow as Cu increases and should be avoided.
  - 3d host + Cu: hollow overbinds while non-hollow over‑weakens; not site-tunable; must switch to Cu‑host side to tune.
  - Rh systems: hollow with coinage too strong; with Cu slightly weak at Rh0.75; with Ni too strong. Limited site data restricts levers to composition.
  - Ru–Cu: highly non‑monotonic and asymmetric; Ru‑rich hollow overbinds, Cu‑rich hollow becomes H‑repulsive. Any optimal window, if present, is likely near mid‑composition.
