가설 수정 결과: Revised hypothesis (v2) based on the new simulation data

What held:
- Site trend is reasonable: hollow remains the strongest H-binding site class for these fcc-like bimetallics (consistent with needing bridge to weaken slightly when hollow is just a bit too strong).
- 25% coinage dilution of Pt-group hosts can place ΔG_H near the −0.27 eV target at hollow sites for certain diluents:
  - Pt–Ag (0.75/0.25, hollow: −0.3025 eV), Pt–Au (−0.2532 eV), Pd–Ag (−0.2911 eV), Rh–Cu (−0.2303 eV) all fall within ±0.05 eV of the target.

What needs correction/refinement:
- Diluents are not interchangeable. For noble/late-4d hosts (Pt, Pd), Cu over-weakens hollow-site H binding at 25% dilution, while Ag and Au are better tuners.
  - Pt–Cu (0.75/0.25, hollow: −0.1527 eV) and Pd–Cu (−0.1589 eV) are too weak, contradicting the original expectation that 25% Cu would land in −0.32 to −0.22 eV.
  - Relative weakening effect (on hollow) for Pt host appears Cu > Au > Ag (Cu gives the weakest binding; Ag maintains the strongest among coinage diluents).
- For strong 3d hosts (Ni, Co), 25% Cu does not sufficiently weaken hollow-site binding; even 50% Cu for Co remains slightly too strong:
  - Ni–Cu (0.75/0.25, hollow: −0.3830 eV), Co–Cu (−0.3832 eV) are too strong; Co–Cu (0.50/0.50, hollow: −0.3274 eV) weakens by only ~0.056 eV from 0.75→0.50, smaller than the originally posited 0.1–0.2 eV.
  - Therefore, tuning Ni/Co with Cu requires either higher Cu fractions (>0.5) and/or moving to weaker sites (bridge/top).

Updated, testable statements:
- Pt/Pd hosts:
  - 25% Ag or Au dilution is optimal at hollow; when ΔG_H is slightly too strong at hollow (e.g., Pt–Ag, Pd–Ag), bridge should tune toward −0.27 eV by +0.02 to +0.08 eV.
  - 25% Cu over-weakens; avoid Pt–Cu and Pd–Cu at hollow for targeting −0.27 eV. If forced to use Pt–Cu/Pd–Cu, increasing the strong-metal fraction beyond 0.75 (if available) would be needed; changing to bridge/top will only weaken further.
- Rh host:
  - 25% Cu already places hollow near target; modest strengthening (beyond hollow) would require higher Rh content (if available) or different coinage diluent (Ag predicted to strengthen vs Cu at the same loading).
- Ni/Co hosts with Cu:
  - 25% Cu is insufficient; 50% Co–Cu hollow is still slightly too strong. Bridge should land closer to −0.27 eV; top could overshoot to too weak.
  - Composition-induced weakening from 0.75→0.50 on hollow is modest (~0.06 eV for Co–Cu), smaller than initially assumed; site choice is a more effective lever here than further equiatomic dilution.
