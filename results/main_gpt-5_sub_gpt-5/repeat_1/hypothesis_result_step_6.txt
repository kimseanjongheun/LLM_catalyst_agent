What held
- Pd + coinage rule strengthened: Pd–Ag, Pd–Cu, and now Pd–Au all show severe non-hollow weakening; hollow is the only viable site on Pd hosts with coinage partners.
- Pt hollow-site diluent order (coinage only) persists: Cu (weakest) > Au > Ag (strongest) at hollow.
- 3d–Cu (Ni/Co–Cu) show very large non-hollow weakening; not site-tunable near −0.27 at 0.75.
- Rh systems remain poorly tunable with current DB coverage: Rh–Cu is slightly weak at hollow; Rh–Ag is too strong at hollow; top/bridge entries that could enable site rescue are missing.

What changed (key corrections)
- Pt host “small-shift” behavior is conditional on the diluent:
  - For coinage diluents (Pt–Ag, Pt–Au), site shifts remain small (tens of meV) and can be non-monotonic (Pt–Au top strengthens vs hollow).
  - For 3d diluents (Pt–Ni), non-hollow can induce very large weakening: Pt–Ni(0.75) h→t = +0.4308 eV, breaking the prior “small-shift” generalization on Pt hosts.
- Pd–Au(0.75) confirms and amplifies Pd+coinage non-hollow weakening: h→b = +0.2171 eV; b→t = +0.3224 eV; h→t = +0.5395 eV (top unbound).
- Rh–Au(0.75) hollow is far too strong (−0.4237 eV); current DB lacks a site lever to correct it.
- Composition trend (Co–Cu hollow): −0.3832 at Co0.75→ −0.3274 at Co0.50 indicates that increasing Cu weakens H binding; linear extrapolation suggests Cu-rich Co–Cu could approach −0.27 if available.

Refined working model
- Site sensitivity is alloy- and ensemble-specific:
  - Pt hosts: With coinage diluents, site shifts are small and can be non-monotonic (top can strengthen or weaken relative to hollow). With 3d diluents, non-hollow can massively weaken H (Pt–Ni example), akin to Pd+coinage behavior.
  - Pd + coinage: Non-hollow sites access coinage-rich ensembles that severely destabilize H; hollow is the only viable site and often still too weak except Pd–Ag.
  - 3d–Cu (Ni/Co–Cu): Non-hollow induces very large weakening; composition tuning toward Cu weakens hollow. Cu-rich compositions might cross −0.27 if accessible.
  - Rh systems: Modest hollow→bridge weakening for Rh–Cu; coinage partners (Ag, Au) are too strong at hollow; DB gaps block site-based rescue.

How these results refine the hypothesis
- The site-ordering rule is conditional. It holds broadly for Pd+coinage and 3d–Cu (non-hollow strongly weakens), but breaks on Pt hosts with coinage (small, sometimes non-monotonic shifts where top can strengthen, e.g., Pt–Au) and fails dramatically on Pt with 3d (Pt–Ni top unbound). Thus:
  - For Pt–Ag and Pt–Au at 0.75, the top site is the working site near −0.28 eV.
  - For Pd–coinage, hollow is the only viable site (non-hollow unusable).
  - For 3d–Cu, non-hollow sites are detrimental; composition (Cu-rich) may be the only viable knob.
  - For Rh–coinage, current DB lacks a site lever; composition changes are likely required.
