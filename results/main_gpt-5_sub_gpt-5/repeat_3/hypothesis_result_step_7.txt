Core claims (updated):
- Pd–W shows strong composition–site antagonism that is Metal1/termination sensitive:
  - Hollow-site binding is minimized near equiatomic Pd–W (−0.2766 eV) but re-strengthens dramatically toward W-rich when W dominates the surface environment (W–Pd-0.75/0.25-hollow = −0.8916 eV). 
  - In contrast, top-site binding weakens with W-rich termination (W–Pd-0.75/0.25-top = +0.312 eV), while Pd-rich Pd–W-0.75/0.25-top/hollow are moderately too strong (−0.328 to −0.362 eV) with small site splitting. Thus, hollow and top respond oppositely to increasing W, and Metal1 (surface termination/ensemble) is decisive.
- Co–Mo maintains a robust, composition-insensitive site bifurcation, intensified when the early T-metal leads:
  - Co–Mo-0.50/0.50: top weak positive (+0.191 eV), bridge/hollow strongly negative (−0.483/−0.593 eV).
  - Co–Mo-0.75/0.25: top +0.215 eV, hollow −0.525 eV; Mo–Co-0.75/0.25: top +0.053 eV, hollow −0.844 eV. Near-target windows are unlikely within DB.
- Coinage dilution + high coordination is not universal; site ordering is pair-specific:
  - Ir–Au (0.75/0.25): hollow moderates to −0.312 eV and top is too weak (+0.362 eV), consistent with “coinage dilution + hollow.”
  - Ir–Ag (0.75/0.25): top is extremely weak (+0.916 eV), bridge is weaker than hollow (−0.300 vs −0.346 eV). Site ordering is hollow < bridge << top, contradicting a “bridge > hollow” expectation.
  - Rh–Au (0.75/0.25): hollow overbinds (−0.424 eV); DB lacks top/bridge here, but current evidence suggests limited site-relief.

Key corrections/updates to v5:
- Pd–W: W-rich does not generally strengthen top sites; instead, top weakens (can become positive) while hollow overbinds strongly. The “higher coordination strengthens binding” shorthand is composition- and termination-dependent.
- Ir–Ag: Bridge is not stronger than hollow at 0.75/0.25; top is far weaker than anticipated. Revise the coordination-compensation motif for this pair.
- Co–Mo: Bifurcation persists and intensifies when Mo leads (Mo–Co), reinforcing deprioritization within the current DB.
