Core claims (updated)
- Pd–W: Site antagonism is ensemble- and termination-sensitive and extends to bridge.
  - Equiatomic Pd–W shows a deep bridge well while hollow is near-thermoneutral (Pd–W-0.50/0.50-bridge = −0.657 eV; hollow = −0.2766 eV). This confirms distinct ensembles control hollow vs bridge.
  - Pd-rich (Pd–W-0.75/0.25) shows a small top–hollow split with both too strong (top −0.3276 eV; hollow −0.3618 eV), consistent with earlier observations.
  - W-rich with W termination (W–Pd-0.75/0.25) preserves extreme antagonism (hollow −0.8916 eV vs top +0.312 eV). Termination dictates the magnitude of site splitting.
  - Practical implication: lowering W fraction under W termination should weaken hollow/top while keeping bridge relatively strong; increasing W under Pd termination should deepen bridge and hollow while top weakens.
- Co–Mo: Robust, composition-insensitive bifurcation persists under both terminations sampled.
  - Co–Mo-0.50/0.50: top weakly positive (+0.191 eV), bridge/hollow strongly negative (−0.483/−0.593 eV).
  - Co–Mo-0.75/0.25: top weakly positive (+0.215 eV), hollow strong (−0.525 eV); bridge missing at this composition in DB.
  - Mo–Co-0.75/0.25: top weak positive (+0.053 eV), hollow very strong (−0.844 eV). A near-optimal window remains unlikely within current DB sampling unless at Mo-poor with Mo termination or at Co-terminated Mo-rich with unusual ensembles.
- Ir–Ag: Coinage dilution does not guarantee bridge > hollow; top is extremely weak at Ir-rich.
  - Ir–Ag-0.75/0.25: top +0.916 eV; hollow modestly stronger than bridge (−0.346 vs −0.300 eV). Site ordering: hollow < bridge << top.
- Ir–Au and Rh–Au: Limited site relief at Ir/Rh-rich; DB coverage sparse at other compositions.
  - Ir–Au-0.75/0.25: hollow near target (−0.312 eV) with top too weak (+0.362 eV); bridge entry absent.
  - Rh–Au-0.75/0.25: hollow overbinds (−0.424 eV); top/bridge absent.
  - Au-rich entries are partially missing (Ir–Au 0.25/0.75 hollow absent), so composition-driven site relief remains unconfirmed.
