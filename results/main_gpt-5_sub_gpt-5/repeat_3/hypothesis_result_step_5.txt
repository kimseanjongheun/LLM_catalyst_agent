- Pd–W: Equiatomic hollow is near-optimal, not overbinding. Pd–W-0.50/0.50-hollow = −0.2766 eV, while Pd–W-0.50/0.50-bridge = −0.657 eV. Thus, at 0.50/0.50 the site hierarchy flips: hollow is much weaker than bridge. The prior rule “higher coordination at 0.50/0.50 overbinds” is overgeneralized—bridge does, hollow does not.
- Pd–W composition–site coupling is non-monotonic. At Pd-rich (0.75/0.25), site sensitivity is weak (top ≈ hollow, both slightly too strong). At 0.50/0.50, site sensitivity is strong and inverted (hollow ≫ weaker than bridge). We hypothesize a convex composition dependence for hollow with a minimum (weakest binding) near equiatomic, and re-strengthening toward the W-rich extreme.

- Ir–Au: Coordination compensation holds (0.75/0.25 hollow near target, top too weak). Database coverage still caps composition tuning.
