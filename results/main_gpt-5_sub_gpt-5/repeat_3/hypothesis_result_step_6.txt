Core claims (updated):
- Pd–W exhibits composition–site coupling with a hollow-site “soft spot” at equiatomic composition: Pd–W-0.50/0.50-hollow = −0.2766 eV (near-optimal), while 0.50/0.50-bridge overbinds (−0.657 eV). We predict a convex dependence of hollow-site H* binding versus composition with a minimum near 0.50/0.50 and re-strengthening toward W-rich.
- Co–Mo maintains a pronounced site bifurcation across tested compositions: top is too weak/positive (0.19–0.22 eV), while bridge/hollow overbind (−0.48 to −0.59 eV). This pair is unlikely to hit −0.27 eV within the DB span and should be deprioritized after closing the W-rich point.
- Coinage dilution + high coordination is pair-specific:
  - Ir–Au: Coordination compensation holds; 0.75/0.25-hollow = −0.312 eV (near, but still stronger than target), top is too weak/positive (+0.362 eV). DB coverage limits further tuning for this pair.

  - Rh–Au: 0.75/0.25-hollow = −0.424 eV, stronger than predicted, falsifying a universal coinage-dilution rule. Thus, coinage identity and the strong-metal partner jointly control H* binding; Rh–Au exhibits enhanced multi-fold binding despite Au dilution.
