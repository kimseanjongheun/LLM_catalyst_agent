Key corrections to the original assumptions:
- S–R bridges at 0.50/0.50 are much too strong. Pd–W-0.50-bridge (−0.657 eV) and Co–Mo-0.50-bridge (−0.483 eV) show that mixed bridge coordination with early refractory metals (Mo, W) overbinds significantly more than predicted. Therefore, bridge on S–R at 0.50 should be deprioritized for targeting −0.27 eV.
- Coinage dilution of very strong binders can over-weaken top sites. Ir–Au-0.75-top gave +0.362 eV, indicating that Au’s ligand/segregation effects suppress Ir’s H* binding far more than anticipated at top sites. Thus, top-on-Ir at 0.75 Ir/0.25 Au should be deprioritized; higher coordination (bridge/hollow) is needed to strengthen adsorption back toward −0.27 eV.

Updated site/composition logic:
- For S–R binaries (S = Pd, Ni, Co; R = Mo, W):
  - Do not expect 0.50/0.50 bridge to cluster near −0.27 eV; it is typically too negative.
  - To approach −0.27 eV, reduce coordination and increase S fraction:
    - Primary: top on S at 0.75(S)/0.25(R).
    - Secondary: bridge at 0.75(S)/0.25(R) only if top remains too weak.
    - Hollow generally overshoots to too negative on S–R; use only if top sites are too weak (unlikely here).
- For very strong S with coinage W (Ir–Au shown):
  - Top on S at 0.75(S)/0.25(Au) can become too weak (positive). To strengthen toward −0.27 eV, increase coordination at the same composition:
    - Primary: bridge at 0.75(S)/0.25(Au).
    - Secondary: hollow at 0.75(S)/0.25(Au) if bridge is still too weak.
    - If composition flexibility exists, 0.50/0.50 with top on S may strengthen relative to 0.75/0.25, but bridge is likely closer than top.

Knowledge accumulated from this iteration:
- Mixed bridge sites with early refractory partners (Mo, W) at 0.50/0.50 overbind substantially; ensemble effects dominate over simple electronic averaging.
- Au exerts a stronger-than-expected downshift on Ir top sites at 0.75/0.25, pushing E_H* into the weak-binding regime (+0.36 eV). Increasing coordination (bridge/hollow) is necessary to recover target-strength adsorption.
- Database coverage is sparse for some originally prioritized S–W pairs (Pt–Cu, Pt–Ni) and for Pd–Mo; however, Ir–Au, Co–Mo, and Pd–W are present and should anchor our next, database-constrained exploration.
