Core claims (updated)
- Pd–W: Ensemble- and termination-sensitive antagonism persists and extends beyond hollow vs top to include bridge.
  - Pd–W-0.50/0.50 exhibits a deep bridge well while hollow is near-thermoneutral (bridge −0.657 eV; hollow −0.2766 eV), confirming distinct ensembles control the two sites.
  - Pd-rich Pd–W-0.75/0.25 retains a small top–hollow split with both too strong (top −0.328 eV; hollow −0.362 eV).
  - W-rich W–Pd-0.75/0.25 (W term.) shows extreme antagonism (hollow −0.892 eV vs top +0.312 eV). Termination dictates the magnitude of site splitting.
  - Practical lever: under W termination, lowering W fraction should weaken hollow/top while keeping bridge relatively strong; under Pd termination, increasing W should deepen bridge and hollow while top weakens.
- Co–Mo: Robust, composition-insensitive bifurcation remains under both terminations sampled.
  - Co–Mo-0.50/0.50: top weakly positive (+0.191 eV), bridge/hollow strongly negative (−0.483/−0.593 eV).
  - Co–Mo-0.75/0.25: top weakly positive (+0.215 eV), hollow strong (−0.525 eV); bridge missing at this composition in DB.
  - Mo–Co-0.75/0.25: top weak positive (+0.053 eV), hollow very strong (−0.844 eV).
- Termination-driven site inversion in coinage pairs (new):
  - Ir–Ag vs Ag–Ir at Ir-rich composition: Ir–Ag-0.75/0.25 (Ir term.) has hollow < bridge << top (−0.346 < −0.300 << +0.916 eV), but Ag–Ir-0.75/0.25 (Ag term.) flips to top binding and hollow repulsive: top −0.549 eV; hollow +0.360 eV. Bridge at this Ag–Ir point is absent in DB.
  - Ir–Au vs Au–Ir at Ir-rich composition: Ir–Au-0.75/0.25 (Ir term.) gives hollow near target (−0.312 eV) and top weak (+0.362 eV), whereas Au–Ir-0.75/0.25 (Au term.) inverts strongly: top binds (−0.469 eV), bridge repulsive (+0.333 eV), hollow repulsive (+0.632 eV).
  - Rh–Au vs Au–Rh at Rh-rich composition: Rh–Au-0.75/0.25 has hollow overbinding (−0.424 eV; top/bridge absent), but Au–Rh-0.75/0.25 (Au term.) suppresses hollow (hollow +0.558 eV) while top/bridge are near thermoneutral (−0.043/−0.063 eV).
  - Implication: coinage termination stabilizes low-coordination H (top/bridge) and destabilizes multi-coordination (hollow), reversing the Ir/Rh-terminated ordering at coinage-rich compositions. This provides a powerful termination lever for site selectivity.
