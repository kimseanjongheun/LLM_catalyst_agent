가설 수정 결과: Modified hypothesis (integrating this batch)

1) Ag as a site-amplifying host for 3d metals: confirmed and generalized
- New success: Ag–Fe 0.50/0.50 bridge = −0.2784 eV (in-window), extending the 50/50 site-inversion rule beyond Ni/Co to Fe.
- Pattern sharpened:
  - When Ag is the host at 50/50, bridge is the tunable, near-target site; hollow is weak/positive (Ag–Ni/Co hollow weak, and we predict Ag–Fe hollow weak/positive).
  - When Ag is the minority dopant in a 3d host (Ni–Ag, Co–Ag at 0.75/0.25), the split is very large but reversed in magnitude: hollow becomes too strong (~−0.37 eV), while bridge is too weak/positive (~0.07–0.12 eV).
Prediction: For Ag–3d at 0.50/0.50, the bridge site robustly targets ΔG_H ≈ −0.27 eV across Ni/Fe and near-target for Co; expect hollow to be slightly positive. For 3d–Ag at 0.75/0.25, expect hollow to be too strong and bridge too weak.

2) Coinage(+Pd) + W at 50/50 hollow remains the high-confidence sweet spot; W dilution strengthens hollow too much
- 50/50 hollow stays in/near window for Ag–W, Pd–W, Cu–W (−0.27 to −0.28 eV).
- New: Diluting W to 0.25 on coinage hosts strengthens hollow substantially (Ag–W: −0.2678 → −0.5803; Cu–W: −0.2817 → −0.5426; Au–W: 0.0418 → −0.3532). Thus, 0.75/0.25 hollow generally overshoots for coinage.
- New: Ag–W 0.75/0.25 bridge = −0.5505 eV confirms bridges remain far too strong upon dilution (magnitude somewhat less extreme than at 50/50 but still unusable).
Prediction: Use 50/50 hollow for coinage(+Pd)+W to hit target; avoid bridges and avoid W-diluted hollows (0.75/0.25).

3) 4d-in-coinage can be ensemble-dominated/site-insensitive
- Ru–Cu 0.75/0.25 hollow vs bridge still differ by only ~0.012 eV (−0.3088 vs −0.2967).
Prediction: For some 4d-in-coinage (e.g., Ru–Cu), expect small site splits; do not assume hollow advantage.

4) Noble + W remains pair-specific; Ir–W keeps its unique ordering
- Ir–W at 50/50: bridge > hollow in weakness (−0.2152 vs −0.3499); at 0.75/0.25 hollow weakens strongly (−0.1666). Others (Pd/Rh/Cu/Ag/Au) show bridges that strongly strengthen relative to hollow.
Prediction: Maintain pair-specific treatment for W; Ir–W is a consistent exception with bridge weaker than hollow.

5) Mo is highly host- and site-contingent (reinforced)
- Cu–Mo: 50/50 hollow near target but sensitive; bridge overshoots (−0.5809); 0.75/0.25 hollow too weak (−0.0587).
- Ag–Mo: both sites too strong at 50/50; 0.75/0.25 hollow extremely weak (+0.8509) → very steep composition dependence.
- Pt–Mo: bridge only modestly strengthens weak hollows at 0.75/0.25 (0.0375 → −0.0665); cannot rescue to target there.
- Ir–Mo: 50/50 bridge strengthens vs hollow (−0.4289 vs −0.3731) but remains too strong; at 0.75/0.25 hollow weakens (−0.1598).
Prediction: Bridge can be a lever on Cu/Ag/Ir hosts (often too strong), but not sufficient on Pt near 0.75/0.25; expect steep composition slopes on coinage hosts.
