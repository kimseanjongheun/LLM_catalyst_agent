Core refinements supported by this batch
- Site amplification on Ag >> Cu at 0.75/0.25 (3d-in-coinage) is confirmed:
  - Ni–Ag and Co–Ag show very large hollow–bridge splits; Cu analogs are sizable but smaller. This validates Ag as a “site-amplifying” host and Cu as “moderately amplifying.”
- Coinage + W at 50/50 hollow remains a robust sweet spot:
  - Ag–W (−0.2678), Pd–W (−0.2766), and Cu–W (−0.2817) all fall in-window or just at the edge; bridge sites overshoot strongly. Avoid W-bridges at 50/50 when targeting ΔG_H ≈ −0.27 eV.
- 4d-in-coinage can be ensemble-dominated:
  - Ru–Cu at 0.75/0.25 is nearly site-insensitive (Δ ≈ 0.012 eV), consistent with a collapsed site effect; this likely persists near 50/50 (DB unavailable to confirm).
- Pair-specific site ordering is real for noble + W:
  - Pd–W, Rh–W: bridge >> hollow (stronger adsorption); Ir–W uniquely reverses this (bridge weaker than hollow).
- Mo is more host- and site-contingent than W:
  - Cu–Mo: bridge strongly strengthens vs hollow at 50/50; Ag–Mo: both sites at 50/50 are too strong; Ir–Mo: bridge > hollow at 50/50.

New contradictions and rule tightening
- Bridge “generally strengthens” is not universal for Mo:
  - Pt–Mo 0.75/0.25 bridge is only −0.0665 eV (from +0.0375 eV on hollow), i.e., a weak correction that fails to pull into the target band. Thus, bridge strengthening for Mo is highly host-dependent and can be modest (Pt) or strong (Cu, Ir).
- W dilution trends are pair-specific and cannot be generalized:
  - Pd–W hollow strengthens upon W dilution (−0.2766 → −0.3618), Ir–W weakens dramatically (−0.3499 → −0.1666), Rh–W weakens modestly (−0.4605 → −0.4128). Maintain pair-specific dilution rules.
