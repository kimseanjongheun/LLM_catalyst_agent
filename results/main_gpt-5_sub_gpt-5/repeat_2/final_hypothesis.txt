최종 가설: Modified hypothesis (integrating this batch)

1) Ag as a site-amplifying host for 3d metals at 50/50: confirmed and generalized to Fe; amplitude ranking emerges
- Confirmations:
  - Ag–Fe 0.50/0.50 bridge = −0.2784 eV (in-window); hollow = +0.0201 eV (slightly positive).
  - Ag–Ni 0.50/0.50 bridge = −0.2908 (in-window); hollow = +0.0247.
  - Ag–Co 0.50/0.50 bridge = −0.2370 (slightly weak); hollow = +0.0440.
- Refined rule:
  - At 0.50/0.50, Ag host inverts site preference for 3d: bridge sits near target (Ni ≈ Fe ≈ −0.28 to −0.29; Co slightly weak), hollow is weak/positive. Expect this to extend to Cr/Mn if present.
  - At 0.75/0.25 with Ag as minority dopant (3d–Ag), the split is large and reversed: hollow is too strong (Ni/Co ≈ −0.37 eV), bridge too weak/positive (≈ +0.07 to +0.12 eV). Predict the same for Fe–Ag.

2) Coinage(+Pd) + W: 50/50 hollow is the robust sweet spot; W dilution over-strengthens; bridges unusably strong
- 50/50 hollow in-window/near-window: Ag–W (−0.268), Pd–W (−0.277), Cu–W (−0.282). Au–W is an outlier (weak, +0.0418).
- W dilution to 0.25 on coinage (+Pd) sharply strengthens hollow: Ag–W (−0.580), Cu–W (−0.543), Au–W (−0.353), Pd–W (−0.362).
- Bridges are far too strong at 50/50 (e.g., Ag–W −0.723; Cu–W −0.747; Pd–W −0.657; Rh–W −0.578; Au–W −0.658) and remain too strong even upon W dilution (Ag–W 0.75/0.25 bridge −0.551).
- Maintain: Use 50/50 hollow for Ag/Cu/Pd+W; avoid bridges and avoid W-diluted hollow.

3) 4d-in-coinage can be ensemble-dominated/site-insensitive
- Ru–Cu 0.75/0.25: hollow vs bridge differ by ~0.012 eV (−0.3088 vs −0.2967). Expect similarly small site splits for select 4d-in-coinage (e.g., Ru–Ag if present); do not assume hollow advantage.

4) Noble + W is pair-specific; Ir–W is a consistent exception
- Ir–W 0.50/0.50: bridge (−0.215) weaker than hollow (−0.350); at 0.75/0.25, hollow weakens further (−0.167). Other nobles (Pd/Rh/Cu/Ag/Au) show bridges much stronger than hollow. Treat Ir–W separately.

5) Mo remains highly host- and site-contingent with steep composition slopes on coinage
- Cu–Mo: 50/50 hollow near-target (−0.297); 50/50 bridge overshoots (−0.581); 0.75/0.25 hollow too weak (−0.059).
- Ag–Mo: 50/50 hollow too strong (−0.603); 0.75/0.25 hollow extremely weak (+0.851) → very steep slope.
- Pt–Mo: at 0.75/0.25, hollow slightly positive (+0.038) and bridge modestly strengthens (−0.066) but cannot reach target there; 50/50 entries appear absent in DB.
- Ir–Mo: 50/50 hollow/bridge both too strong (−0.373/−0.429); 0.75/0.25 hollow weakens (−0.160).
- Implication: On coinage hosts, Mo requires tight composition control; bridges often over-strengthen and do not reliably “rescue” weak hollows on Pt near 0.75/0.25.

6) Au is not Ag-like for 3d at 50/50
- Au–Fe 0.50/0.50: hollow very weak (+0.320), bridge only moderately strengthened (−0.124), well short of target. Expect Au–Ni/Au–Co 50/50 bridges to be similarly too weak compared to Ag analogs.
