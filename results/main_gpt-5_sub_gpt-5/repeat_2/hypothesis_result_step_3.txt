What held up and how it refines our rules
- Hollow strengthens substantially vs bridge. For 3d-rich ensembles the hollow→bridge shift can be ~0.25–0.30 eV. This remains the most powerful lever to correct under-/over-binding, but the magnitude is pair-dependent.
- Weak-binder primaries (coinage) require early transition metals and high coordination to approach −0.27 eV, but “coinage + Mo/W at 50/50 hollow” is not universally robust; the identity of the early partner matters strongly.

Key updates to the hypothesis
1) Strong-binder 3d primaries diluted in late 3d hosts (Ni/Co in Cu) at 0.75/0.25 are extremely site-sensitive:
- Mixed bridge is too weak for Ni (−0.082 eV).
- Mixed hollow overshoots for both Ni–Cu and Co–Cu (≈ −0.383 eV).
- Therefore, the optimum for Ni/Co-in-Cu likely lies at intermediate composition or at bridge for Co (but not Ni), rather than a universal “move to hollow” prescription.

2) Mo vs W as the early partner at 0.75/0.25 hollow depends on the primary:
- Pd, Rh, Ir: W strengthens relative to Mo (more negative ΔEH). Overshoots (Pd–W, Rh–W) can be corrected by reducing W fraction or lowering site coordination.
- Pt (and coinage): W weakens relative to Mo. For Pt, increasing early-metal loading (to ~50/50) with Mo, not W, is the right direction to strengthen binding toward −0.27 eV. Ir shows only a small W–Mo difference.

3) Coinage + early at 50/50 hollow:
- W is generally “safer” than Mo for Ag/Cu: Ag–W (−0.268 eV) is optimal; Cu–W (−0.282 eV) is very close; Cu–Mo (−0.297 eV) is slightly over-strong.
- Ag–Mo is far too strong (−0.603 eV), while Au–W is too weak (+0.042 eV). Au likely needs Mo (or higher early-metal fraction) to reach target.

Cross-cutting site guidance
- Use hollow when adsorption is too weak, but expect large strengthening for 3d-rich ensembles (risk of overshoot).
- Use bridge to weaken overly strong hollow cases if composition tuning is unavailable.

Revised, testable hypothesis (actionable)
- For Ni/Co-in-Cu at 0.75/0.25, hollow over-strengthens; Co–Cu at bridge may reach −0.27 eV, while Ni–Cu likely remains too weak at bridge.
- For Pd/Rh/Ir, W > Mo in strengthening; reduce W fraction or coordination to correct overshoot. For Pt (and coinage), Mo ≥ W in strengthening; use Mo and/or increase early-metal fraction.
- For coinage at 50/50 hollow, favor W for Ag/Cu; use Mo for Au (or increase early-metal loading) to correct under-binding.
