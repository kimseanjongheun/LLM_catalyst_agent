Summary of what held up and how it refines our rules
- Ag amplifies site sensitivity far more than Cu for 3d-in-coinage at 0.75/0.25:
  - Ni–Ag: bridge +0.0725 vs hollow −0.3703 eV (Δ ≈ −0.443 eV)
  - Co–Ag: bridge +0.1177 vs hollow −0.3658 eV (Δ ≈ −0.483 eV)
  - Ni–Cu: bridge −0.0821 vs hollow −0.3830 eV (Δ ≈ −0.301 eV)
  - Co–Cu: bridge −0.0342 vs hollow −0.3832 eV (Δ ≈ −0.349 eV)
  This confirms “site amplification” is extreme on Ag and substantial on Cu.
- W in coinage confirms “dilution strengthens” and bridge over-strengthens:
  - Cu–W: 50/50 hollow −0.2817 (near-target), 75/25 hollow −0.5426, 50/50 bridge −0.7466
  - Ag–W: 50/50 hollow −0.2678 (on-target), 75/25 hollow −0.5803, 50/50 bridge −0.7227
  - Au–W: 50/50 hollow +0.0418 (too weak), 75/25 hollow −0.3532, 50/50 bridge −0.6581
- 4d-in-coinage can be site-insensitive (ensemble-dominated):
  - Ru–Cu 0.75/0.25: bridge −0.2967 vs hollow −0.3088 (Δ ≈ −0.012 eV)
- Noble + W: pair- and site-specific ordering is validated at 50/50
  - Pd–W: bridge −0.6570 << hollow −0.2766 (bridge much stronger)
  - Rh–W: bridge −0.5776 < hollow −0.4605 (bridge stronger)
  - Ir–W: bridge −0.2152 > hollow −0.3499 (bridge weaker, unique among W pairs)
- Mo behavior is host- and site-dependent
  - Cu–Mo: 50/50 hollow −0.2971 vs 50/50 bridge −0.5809 (bridge strongly strengthens)
  - Ag–Mo: 50/50 hollow −0.6029 vs bridge −0.6168 (bridge slightly strengthens)
  - Ag–Mo dilution extreme: 75/25 hollow +0.8509 eV (severe weakening)
  - Ir–Mo: 50/50 bridge (−0.4289) strengthens vs hollow (−0.3731); 75/25 hollow is too weak (−0.1598)

New contradictions and refined rules
- “Bridge weakens relative to hollow” is not general:
  - Many systems show bridge strengthens substantially (Pd–W, Rh–W, Cu–W, Cu–Mo, Ir–Mo, Ag–Co at 50/50).
  - Ir–W remains an exception with bridge weaker than hollow at 50/50.
- Composition responses for W are not universal across noble hosts:
  - Pd–W strengthens upon W dilution (50/50 hollow −0.2766 → 75/25 hollow −0.3618), opposite to Ir–W (weakens to −0.1666) and different from Rh–W (modest weakening upon dilution).
- 3d-in-coinage at 50/50 requires pair-specific site choice:
  - Co–Cu 50/50 hollow is slightly strong (−0.3274) but bridge variant is absent in DB; this limits tunability.
  - Ag–Co 50/50 bridge lands near target (−0.2370), while hollow is too weak (+0.0440), indicating a site inversion at 50/50 on Ag.
