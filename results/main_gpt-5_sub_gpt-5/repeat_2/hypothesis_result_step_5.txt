What held up and how it refines our rules
- Site amplification for 3d-in-coinage remains extreme and host-dependent, with Ag >> Cu:
  - Ni–Ag and Co–Ag at 0.75/0.25 show huge hollow–bridge shifts (≈ −0.44 to −0.48 eV), confirming Ag amplifies site sensitivity beyond Cu.
  - 3d-in-Cu shows substantial but smaller shifts (e.g., Ni–Cu ≈ −0.30 eV).
- Weak-binder coinage hosts need early-transition partners and high coordination to strengthen H adsorption, but composition responses are pair- and host-specific:
  - Mo in coinage: dilution weakens, consistent in Cu (−0.297 → −0.059 eV) and dramatically so in Ag (−0.603 → +0.851 eV).
  - W in coinage: dilution strengthens strongly across Cu, Ag, and Au (e.g., Cu–W −0.282 → −0.543 eV; Ag–W −0.268 → −0.580 eV; Au–W +0.042 → −0.353 eV).
- Pd/Rh/Ir with W at hollow: W > Mo in strengthening still holds, but bridge can be much stronger than hollow for Pd and Rh at 50/50 (contrary to “bridge weakens” heuristic).
- Pt with early metals: Mo > W in strengthening holds, but Pt–W remains too weak even at 50/50 hollow (+0.003 eV). Pt–Mo 50/50 is absent; the Pt–early path likely requires >50% early metal or different site motifs not present in DB.

New contradictions and refined rules
- “Bridge weakens relative to hollow” is not general. It can invert:
  - W-containing systems at 50/50 show bridge >> hollow for Pd–W (−0.657 vs −0.277), Rh–W (−0.578 vs −0.461), and Cu–W (−0.747 vs −0.282). Ir–W is the exception (bridge −0.215 < hollow −0.350).
  - Ag–Mo 50/50 bridge slightly strengthens vs hollow (−0.617 vs −0.603), i.e., bridge does not systematically weaken.
- 4d-in-coinage can be weakly site-sensitive at 0.75/0.25:
  - Ru–Cu bridge −0.297 vs hollow −0.309 (Δ ≈ −0.012 eV), contradicting our expected large hollow gain. Here the ensemble dominates over site.
- Composition sensitivity can be extreme and non-monotonic in Ag:
  - Ag–Mo 50/50 hollow is very strong (−0.603 eV), but Ag–Mo 75/25 hollow becomes extremely weak (+0.851 eV), far exceeding Cu’s weakening upon Mo dilution.

Updated, testable rules by family
- 3d-in-coinage:
  - At 0.75/0.25, bridge is too weak and hollow overshoots (especially in Ag). The route to target is composition and site tuning near 50/50 with careful site choice. Co–Cu is more promising than Ni–Cu at 50/50.
- 4d-in-coinage:
  - Site effects can collapse (Ru–Cu), so map both sites before assuming hollow helps. Bridge near −0.30 eV may already be optimal for certain 4d.
- Noble + W:
  - W > Mo persists for Pd/Rh/Ir at hollow, but site ordering flips for Pd and Rh at 50/50 (bridge >> hollow); Ir is opposite (bridge < hollow). Expect strong pair- and site-specific behavior.
- Noble + Mo:
  - Strengthening slope with composition can be steep (Ir–Mo 50/50 hollow overshoots to −0.373). Use bridge to de-strengthen where available.
- Coinage + early:
  - W: dilution strengthens across Cu/Ag/Au and bridge often over-strengthens at 50/50. Avoid bridge for W if targeting −0.27 eV.
  - Mo: dilution weakens; in Ag the weakening is extreme. Only 50/50 is viable for Ag–Mo, but it overshoots at both hollow and bridge.
